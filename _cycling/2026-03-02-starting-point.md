At this point I do not have impressive pictures of cycling adventures under my belt. This post serves as a reminder of me of my starting point and what my goals are for this exciting sport.

## Cycling — how it started

I have never been big into cycling up until graduating UBC in 2025. After joining my first job I quickly realised that cycling is a big part of life for many of my colleages. This positive peer presure is what boosted my excitement about this hobby. I really wanted to try biking to and from work. The only caveat is that I live in Richmond and work downtown Vancouver. I have never biked such distances before and it seemed near impossible.

Luckily, I was able to find enough inner-strength to start pedaling on the stationary bike at my building's gym. I started light with a 20 minute excersize and over the course of a month I was able to build up to 45 minutes of consistent pedaling. This gave me enough confidence to take my aluminun hybrid bike and go to work!

The first couple of weeks have been hell. My legs and cardio system were not comfortable with such a demanding activity. However, I pushed through and started consistently cycling 2 days per week, covering about 36kms each day. I felt very proud of myself and I quickly started seeing improvements to my physical health.

## New bike

Soon after my first several rides to work, I discovered that a colleage and friend of mine is selling his carbon fibre road bike. Now, given the prices for those bikes, I did not think I would ever buy one for myself. The timing, however, was too perfect. Adrian was also very excitied about my cycling journey and sold me his amazing Giant TCR Advanced road bike at a great deal. Adrian, I owe most of my progress and excitment about this sport to you. Thank you! For those curios, here is a poorly taken picture of the bike that does not do it justice.  

![Bike](/assets/images/cycling/001/bike.jpeg)

## Current Activity

Now it has been 8 months since the "start" of my cycling journey and I still maintain a 2-3 day a week ride in to work. See the strava routes in the gallery. I am happy that I am able to keep up with regular cycling but recently I started wanting more. The longest of my cycling trips has only been 36km. Seeing others go on mutliday trips across Europe and North America really made me feel like I am missing out on a big part of what cycling has to offer. After discovering mutliday cycling routes around Vancouver Island on  [(__Bikepacking.com__)](https://bikepacking.com/), I made it my goal to get out with my friends and do a two day trip on nothing but bicycles. 

## What's next

At this point, my main goals are to maintain my cycling fitness and bully a couple of my friends to go on an overnight trip. Luckily, my friends are up for anything so it will not take much convincing. I am hoping to post a lot more photos and stories about my cycling adventures solo and with friends. And if not, this point will keep staring at me judgementally until I do. 

> Nothing more than a bike, open roads, friends and positive self-talk
